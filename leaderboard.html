How Scores Work

Scores are calculated differently for each game:

  • Reaction Speed: Faster clicks get higher points
  • Memory Matching: Fewer attempts and faster time = higher score
  • Puzzle Slider: Fewer moves and faster time = higher score
  • Trivia Quiz: Correct answers with speed bonuses
